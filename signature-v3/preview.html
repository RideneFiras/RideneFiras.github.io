04 / notes

Before first use: push this folder so the GIF resolves at https://firasridene.tech/signature-v3/fr-matrix.gif.
The card background and the GIF background are the same #08080b, so the image has no visible edge.
Dark-mode clients may try to invert the card. The GIF cannot be inverted, so if that bothers you, v1 in signature/ is the light-background option.
To paste: open signature.html, select all, copy, paste into your mail client's signature editor.
